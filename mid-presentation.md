Mid-project Report (Upload Due Oct 19 11:59pm ET in gradescope):
 
Prepare a PDF of up to 4 pages in NeurIPS format, excluding references. Organize the report using the following sections:
Problem and Motivation: What research question are you addressing, and why does it matter?
Previous Work and Its Limitations: What work are you building on? What challenges or gaps does it leave unresolved?
Proposed Method: Describe your approach and your contribution beyond the existing work.
Experiment Setups:
Backbone Models: Which models will you use, and how will you adapt or use them?
Datasets: What data will you use for training, validation, and evaluation, as applicable? Describe any necessary data collection or preparation.
Evaluation: Which baselines will you compare against? What experiments or ablations will assess your contribution?
Metrics: How will you measure performance and determine whether your approach succeeds?
Current Progress: What have you completed? Include preliminary results or examples if available, and describe any obstacles.
Final Deliverables and Plan: What will you deliver by the end of the course; e.g., code, a dataset, a demo, or an experimental analysis? Include a brief timeline for the remaining work and, for teams, the division of responsibilities.
